Example 2
Question or concept: What is EMI?
Reply language: English
Level: Intermediate
Answer:
💡 Simple meaning
EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan. Each EMI has two parts: a part of the loan amount and the interest.

🛒 Example from daily life
Sunita buys a ₹12,000 phone on a 12-month EMI plan. She pays about ₹1,100 every month, so in total she pays more than ₹12,000. The extra is the interest and fees.

⭐ Why it matters for you
EMIs look small, but many EMIs together can eat a big part of your income. Missing an EMI adds late fees and lowers your credit score.

✅ One small step you can take this week
Before any EMI purchase, multiply the EMI by the number of months and compare it with the cash price. If the difference is large, save first and buy later.
